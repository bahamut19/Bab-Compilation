BB00024: Du’a’ li-Huruf-i-Bismi’llahi’r-Rahmani’r-Rahim

Part 1: “Glorified art Thou, O my God! Thou art…”

In the name of God, the Most Unapproachable, the Most Holy

Glorified art Thou, O my God! Thou art the Creator of the heavens and the earth and whatsoever lieth between them, their Ordainer, their Fashioner, their Originator, and their Author. Thou hast ever been the One God, Single, Self-Subsistent, the Ever-Living, the Self-Sustaining. Thou hast taken unto Thyself neither consort nor son, and Thou hast created all things through Thy power and established the heavens and the earth and whatsoever lieth between them through Thy might. Nothing escapeth the dominion of Thy oneness, nor is anything concealed from the sovereignty of Thy Self-Subsistence, nor doth anything in the kingdoms of the heavens and the earth and whatsoever lieth between them elude Thy power.

Thou hast transcended all things through the essential nature of Thy Being, and hast exalted Thyself through the pure essence of Thy Reality above the height of all things. Thou hast risen through the abstraction of Thy Self above the loftiness of all things, and hast established Thyself through the essence of Thy Identity above the inaccessibility of all things. Thou art the Most High, and all besides Thee bow down before Thee in worship and glorification and praise and sanctification. Thou art the Most Exalted, and all beneath Thy gaze extol Thy majesty and greatness and might and oneness.

Glorified art Thou, O my God! There is no moment when any can be associated with the sovereignty of Thy holiness and the dominion of Thy singleness. How could I ever aspire to ascend unto the heaven of Thy sanctified glory or rise unto the clouds of Thy exalted names? Ever hath Thy Reality praised Thy Reality, and all else doth praise Thee within the bounds of Thy sovereign might. Forever dost Thou magnify Thy Self through Thy Self, and should any besides Thee magnify Thee, it is but within the confines of the geometry Thou hast established through the limitations of Thy creation and brought into being through the manifestations of Thy might.

O my God, by such likeness are all likenesses and similitudes made known; and by such balance, O my Lord, are all geometries and names weighed. Thou art the Most Exalted, and unto the height of Thy loftiness do all ascend and before Thee bow the knee. Thou art the All-Transcending, and unto the summit of Thy exaltation do all lift their gaze and, standing in Thy presence, glorify Thy Name.

What, then, is the splendor of Thy beauty, above every beauty in the realm of origination! How august Thy majesty, surpassing every majesty within the domain of invention! How ravishing the comeliness of Thy countenance, and how sublime the glances of the eyes turned toward Thy Face among the kings of earth and heaven! How immeasurable the elevation of the sanctity of Thy self-subsisting oneness amid the billows of names and attributes! How radiant the effulgences of Thy unity, with Thy peerlessness, upon the surging seas of glory and detachment!

From everlasting hast Thou stood, single and alone, upon the throne of Thine essential might—far above all that Thou hast, by Thy power, brought forth. And forever wilt Thou remain, invincible and exalted, beyond all over whom Thou hast, by Thy sovereignty, conferred dominion. Every thing deriveth a sign of Thy glory; yet none among Thy creatures can hymn Thy praise as becometh the sublimity of Thy praise—though all things yearn to celebrate Thee according to the loftiness of Thy glorification. None can worthily laud Thee, for the eminence of Thy thanksgiving is unapproachable—albeit all things long to extol Thee, whilst Thy praise remaineth beyond their reach. None can truly unify Thee, for the dominion of Thy peerless oneness is self-sufficient—though all things yearn to acknowledge Thee, even as Thou hast affirmed Thine Essence by the altitude of Thine eternity.

None among Thy servants can magnify Thee as is meet, for the ascendant might of Thy majestic grandeur is insurmountable, yet all that dwell from the highest height unto the primal atom fain would exalt Thee according to the excellence of Thy majesty, the sublimity of Thy singleness, the loftiness of Thy self-subsistence, and the holiness of Thy radiant sanctity.

Thou art the Being who by Thy Will hast given being to all things; the Essence who by Thy purpose hast brought all things into being; the Light who by Thy power hast decreed all things; the All-Knowing who by Thy lordly knowledge hast numbered all things; the Fashioner who by the wondrous artistry of Thy gracious lordship hast formed all things. How resplendent is Thy glory, O my God, in the depths of might and detachment, and how sublime is Thy majesty, O my Beloved, in the kingdom of might and inaccessibility. How beauteous is the beauty of Thy holy Countenance in its bounty within the holy realms of might and majesty, and how delicate are the wondrous manifestations of the favors of Thy grandeur in the ultimate essences of might and joy.

I have ever glorified Thee through the essence of my being, certain that this befitteth not the majesty of Thy holy praise, and bearing witness that this befitteth not the loftiness of Thy holy sanctification. And in like manner do I praise Thee, O my Beloved, and proclaim Thy unity, O my Desire, and magnify Thee, O Thou to Whom I shall return and in Whom I find my resting-place. Thou hast ever clothed me in every vesture of Thy might, wherein Thou didst cause whomsoever Thou wouldst to hearken a hundredfold times; and all this within the Kingdom of the might of the sanctity of Thy loving care. And Thou ceaselessly settest in motion the ordinances of Thy subsistence, as Thou dost will. I know of naught that grieveth my knowledge before the Sovereign of Thy holy oneness. There ceaselessly rain down from the heaven of the bounty of Thy Lordship the rains of Thy wondrous mercy, and there unceasingly descend from the heaven of Thy holy love the outpourings of the wondrous effects of the manifestation of Thy power.

Can any whom Thou art his God be grieved by aught? Glory be unto Thee! Glory be unto Thee! Can any whom Thou art his Lord […] from his knowledge of aught? Glory be unto Thee! Glory be unto Thee! Can any whom Thou art his Creator be rendered powerless by aught? Glory be unto Thee! Glory be unto Thee! Can any whom Thou art his Possessor have his dominion diminished by aught? Glory be unto Thee! Glory be unto Thee! Can any whom Thou art his Sovereign be associated with aught? Glory be unto Thee! Glory be unto Thee!

Through my recognition of Thy oneness shall I glory over all who dwell in the kingdom of Thy earth and the heaven of Thy Lordship, and through my acknowledgment of Thy oneness shall I rise above all the inhabitants of Thy realm. Is there any glory, O my God, save in submission to Thy oneness, and is there any exaltation, O my God, save in prostration before the Sovereign of Thy might? Nay, by Thy might! Through my humility before Thee shall I be raised above all who are exalted, and through my submissiveness before Thy eyes shall I ascend above all who are high.

Thine is the decree in its essence, and the praise in its purity, and the beginning in its pristine nature. Thou dost ever […] the people of Thy love with the holy fragrances of Thy attraction, and Thou causest to ascend whom Thou willest through what Thou bestowest upon them of the wondrous manifestations of Thy power to the horizon of the atmosphere of Thy love. Thou art He through connection with Whom none can be brought low, and through separation from Whose exalted sovereignty none can find independence.

Therefore unto Thee, O my God, […] all from the highest heights of the essence of my being to the lowest depths of the clay of my reality, for I witness as absolute truth and observe as the very essence of certainty that the clay which Thou hast attributed to Thyself in Thy Book causeth the necks of all who are mighty to bow down before it and the mightiest of the mighty to circle round it. The body which beforetime bore witness to all Thy tribulations—how do all the great ones among the mighty prostrate themselves before it and all the exalted ones among the wealthy humble themselves before it! If this be the effect of clay’s connection to Thee and the fruit of the body that was martyred in Thy path […] how much more so for him whom Thou hast chosen for the loftiness of Thy sanctification and […] for the exaltation of Thy glorification, whom Thou hast singled out for the elevation of Thy unity and preferred above all things for the inaccessibility of Thy magnification.

Glory be unto Thee! There is no God but Thee, without my looking to aught else or […] […] in aught save Thy unity. Glory be unto Thee! There is no God but Thee, without my heart finding rest in aught else or my being finding stability in any but Thee. Glory be unto Thee, O God, through Thy remembrance of me.

I shall verily glory over all who are in the Kingdom of Names and Attributes, and through My remembrance of Thee shall I triumph over all who are in the kingdoms of earth and heaven. And in that time, O my God, shall I soar in the atmosphere of Thy divine Unity, and wing my flight in the heights of Thy sovereignty, and praise Thee for what Thou art in the horizon of Thy dominion and kingdom. For he who seeketh Thee in the heights of Thy names, or desireth to tread the paths of Thy glory and transcendence, would not wish to descend from the heaven of Thy names to […] Thy creation or to be like Thy servants, for there all names are cut off and all likenesses are forbidden.

And I love Thy manifestation at the Point which Thou hast established in the Bayan through Thy creative power. Through Thee shall I behold Thee, and testify before Thee to that which Thou hast caused me to witness concerning Thyself, that Thou art God, there is no God but Thee, alone, without partner. Thine is the creation and the command, and Thine are might and transcendence, and Thine are power and exaltation, and Thine are joy and gladness, and Thine are grandeur and majesty, and Thine are sovereignty and splendor, and Thine are might and radiance, and Thine are the names and whatsoever Thou hast loved in Thy knowledge of the unseen realms of divine good-pleasure and the manifold wonders of Thy decree.

And I love to acknowledge the right of […] before Thee in the depths of Thy names, and to behold above the heads of all the manifestations of Thy likenesses, inasmuch as He hath preceded all Thy names through what Thou hast cast upon Him of Thy majesty and the sublimity of Thy transcendence.

And I shall say: O Thou Who art the Most Exalted above all that is exalted, O Thou Who art the Most Glorious above all possessed of glory, O Thou Who art the Most Holy above all possessed of transcendence, O Thou Who art the Most Elevated above all possessed of elevation, O Thou Who art the Most Great above all possessed of greatness! Is there any manifestation other than Thee, O my God, whereby I might be guided to Thee? Is there any remembrance besides Thee, O my Beloved, whereby I might remember Thee? Glorified art Thou! There is no God but Thee. Thou art the First before all things, and the Last after all things, and the Outward above all things, and the Inward beneath all things, and the Omnipotent over all things.

Exalted is Thy Being above description through similitudes of glory and majesty, and sanctified is Thy Essence from praise through manifestations of glory and beauty. Thou hast ever been one God, Single, Eternal, Independent, Ever-Living, Self-Subsisting. Thou hast taken neither consort nor son, nor hath Thy Being been joined with aught that Thou hast created or determined or fashioned or limited or numbered. The wonders of the signs of Thy power have ever shone forth upon the people of Thy dominion, and the evidences of Thy divine Unity have ever been raised up among those who dwell in the kingdom of Thy earth and heaven.

How shall I beseech Thee, O my God, by the Name which Thou hast made to precede all Thy names, and through which Thou hast brought into being what was and what will be until the end of Thy creation’s term, and through which Thou hast manifested those who dwell in the kingdom of Thy creation and Thy command! Verily Thou art He Who hath ever been and will ever be […] to Him through it from before when there was no beginning to when Thou didst manifest, and no limit to when Thou shalt manifest. Thine is the might and power, O Thou Who art possessed of strength and might! And Thine are grandeur and exaltation, O Thou Who art possessed of sovereignty and transcendence! And Thine are awe and detachment, O Thou Who art possessed of joy and gladness! And Thine are majesty and glory, O Thou Who art possessed of countenance and magnificence! And Thine are creation and command in the kingdom of origination, O Thou Who art possessed of ascents and attributes! And Thine are dominion and kingdom, O Thou Who art possessed of bounties!

O Ever-Bountiful One! The Sovereign of Thy hallowed munificence hath ever showered the rain of Thy lordly majesty from the heaven of Thy holy oneness upon those who dwell in the kingdom of Thy earth and the heaven of Thy loving-kindness. And forever shall the dawning-places of the command of the kingdom of the wondrous innovation of Thy lordship manifest that which Thou hast ordained in the kingdom of the might of Thy dominion through the marvelous handiwork of Thy transcendent unity. How exalted is Thy sublimity in the depths of names and similitudes, and how resplendent is Thy loftiness in the kingdom of might and majesty! Thou hast taken unto Thyself neither consort nor child; Thou hast had no partner in the Kingdom, nor any guardian upon the earth save by Thy leave. They who are in the kingdom of names and attributes shall glorify Thee for the loftiness of Thy glorification; they who are in the kingdom of earth and heaven shall praise Thee for the unapproachableness of Thy praise; they who are in the dominions of beginning and ending shall confess Thine oneness for the self-subsistence of Thy unity; and they who are in the depths of origins and ends shall magnify Thee for the joy of the gladness of the countenance of the King of the holiness of Thy Lordship.

Thou art the Being before all things through the existence of Thy essential reality; Thou art the Being after all things through the simplicity of Thy soul; Thou art the Creator of all things through the pure abstraction of Thine “I-ness”; and Thou art the Essence above all things through the camphor-sweetness of Thine eternity. None can know Thee save Thyself, and none can proclaim Thine oneness but Thyself. Ever dost Thou draw them that dwell in the kingdoms of Thine earth and heaven through the affairs of Thy wondrous gifts and Thy bounties, and ever dost Thou uplift them that dwell in the kingdom of Thine earth and the heaven of Thy care unto the most exalted horizon through the marvels of the traces of the dawning of that which shineth from the horizon of Thine unseen and riseth from the daysprings of the Sun of Thy judgment.

Nothing escapeth Thy knowledge—neither in the kingdom of earth nor of heaven nor in whatsoever lieth between them; nor is aught beyond Thy power—neither in the realm of command nor of creation nor in whatsoever is beneath them. Thou art He Who hath created all things by Thy will, upheld the heavens and the earth and whatsoever lieth between them by Thy power, and … Thy paradise upon all who are in the kingdom of Thine earth and the heaven of Thy care, and completed Thy proof upon every atom of Thy realm. Thine, O my God, is the Most Exalted Height above every height and loftiness, and Thine, O my God, is the Most Glorious Beauty above every sublimity and splendour. Sanctified are Thy names above likeness and similitude, and exalted are Thy manifestations above design and allusion.

Send down, then, O my God, at that time, from the marvels of the oceans of Thy bounty and Thy gifts, and from the surging billows of the sea of Thy generosity and Thy decrees, that wherewith every thing may be enriched at the appearance of Thy effulgence, and whereby every thing may attain unto that whereunto the bird of its heart ascendeth from the dawning-place of Thine unseen nurture. There is no power and no might save through Thee; no command save from Thy presence; no honour save in Thy good-pleasure. No portion hath he whose will is not beneath Thy will—inasmuch as, if he remember Thee not in the depths of Thy names, nor stand before the King of Thy majesty, then send down, O my God, at that time, by the breezes of Thy forgiveness, upon them that dwell in the kingdoms of Thine earth and heaven.

For he who obeyeth Thee profiteth Thee not, nor addeth aught unto Thy dominion; and he who veil eth himself from Thy command cannot thwart Thee, nor diminish aught of Thy sovereignty. Glorified art Thou: there is none other God but Thee. This is honour for this one, and this is abasement for that one; and there is no power and no might save through Thee. Verily Thou art the Best of the merciful.
