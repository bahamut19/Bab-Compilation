BB00043: Answer to a letter from Dayyan

Part 1: “Glory be unto Thee, O my God! I…”

In the Name of God, the Most Unapproachable, the Most Holy

Glory be unto Thee, O my God! I testify, and all things testify, that Thou art God, and that there is no God but Thee, alone with no partner. Thine is the Kingdom and the Dominion, Thine the Might and the Omnipotence, Thine the Power and the Divinity, Thine the Strength and the Sovereignty, Thine the Majesty and the Glory, Thine the Direction and the Perfection, Thine the Countenance and the Beauty, Thine the Mercy and the Bounty, Thine the Authority and Justice, Thine the Similitude and Examples, Thine the Stations and Exaltation, Thine the Power and Action, Thine the Similitude and Examples, Thine the Grandeur and Pride, Thine the Might and Elevation, Thine the Glory and Invincibility, Thine the Loftiness and Ascendancy, Thine the Joy and Gladness, Thine the Blessings and Favors, and Thine is whatsoever Thou hast loved from the treasuries of Thy Command and the repositories of Thy Knowledge.

Glorified art Thou! There is no God but Thee. Thou hast ever been One God, Single, Eternal, Self-Subsisting, Ever-Living, Self-Sustaining. Thou hast taken neither consort nor son, and Thou hast had no partner in Thy creation, nor any protector in what Thou hast fashioned. Thou hast ordained all things by Thy Will and formed all things by Thy Purpose. Thou hast ever been omnipotent over all souls, doing as Thou willest. Thou givest life and causest death, then causest death and givest life, and verily Thou art the Ever-Living Who dieth not, the Sovereign Who passeth not away, the Just Who wrongeth not, the Ruler Who changeth not, and the Single One from Whose grasp nothing escapeth, neither in the heavens nor on the earth nor between them. Thou createst what Thou willest by Thy Command. Verily, Thou art powerful over all things.

How can I glorify Thee when all stand by Thy Command, and how can I sanctify Thee when all speak Thy praise, by virtue of the sublimity of Thy majesty, the exaltation of Thy might, the elevation of Thy radiance, and the transcendence of Thy glory? Thine are the Most Beautiful Names in their essence and the Most Exalted Examples in their abstraction. I testify concerning that which hath been revealed from Him Whose name, when the doubled lam is not counted, equateth to a word of God, and Who hath ascended unto Thee with the emotions of His heart as best He could, mentioning before Thee such of Thy Most Beautiful Names as were revealed aforetime. I testify, and all Thy creation testifieth, that unto Thee belong all the Most Beautiful Names—their first and their last, their manifest and their hidden—and the Most Exalted Examples in their essence, their pristine nature, their simplicity and their abstraction.

There is no doubt that Thou hast sent the Messengers to manifest Thy grace and bounty, and revealed the Books to exalt Thy Word and Thy sovereignty in Thy most exalted heights, where all that is lofty boweth down before Thy Countenance. How wondrous is Thy transcendence, where all that is sublime submitteth to Thee through what Thou hast revealed unto it within itself. I shall glorify Thee beyond all that hath glorified Thee or shall glorify Thee, and I shall sanctify Thee beyond all that hath sanctified Thee or shall sanctify Thee. According to what hath been revealed in His Book, it behooveth that mention be made in the names of the Bayan, and that I be gathered unto the most exalted heights of Paradise. He hath mentioned from His tongue what they asked of Him—conditions which Thou hadst detailed aforetime—and Thou hast closed the doors against those who sought Thee through what Thou hadst made clear from Thy presence. Verily, that which He hath mentioned was revealed in the words of Thy martyrs aforetime, where they declared that Thy Proof would appear from Mecca in such wise as was mentioned.

Glory be unto Thee and exalted art Thou! I have loved all that was or will be through a single word which Thou didst reveal aforetime, that there is no doubt that the Qur’an is Thy Book, whereby it is proven that this is Thy Book, confirming what Thou hast revealed unto me of Thy verses. Thus by this is it proven unto him who is certain that it is Thy Book that this is indeed Thy Book and there is no God but Thee, and it was proven aforetime in the Qur’an that none other than Thee could reveal a single verse.

Since thou hast established this for everyone who hath followed it, when thou speakest in the manner thou hast spoken, no one can imagine that it is from other than thee, until, when thou sayest unto him “Am I not thy Lord?”, he can but answer “Yea.”

Thus is established what thou didst intend, for in those days the people derived the proofs of their religion from what their rightful divines referred back to their Imams, and their traditions, if they conflicted not with the Book of their Prophet, were permitted to be acted upon in their religion; otherwise they needs must return it to its source.

And concerning that which was asked of me, I wish to answer it with drops from the oceans, that they may become true manifestations unto him. For the Sacred House standeth through the word of Thy Beloved of old, and its existence was solely by Thy command. Thus, when Thy Cause is made manifest, the like thereof shall appear, even though the essence of the Sacred House returneth to mere clay. This progresseth even as Thy House was once the Holy Temple, then when Thou didst exalt it, Thou madest it the Sacred House, then when Thou didst further exalt it, Thou madest it whatsoever Thou didst will by Thy command. And this is true of all limitations, for though the honor of the earth lieth in what resteth upon it, it hath in itself no elevation save through that which is upon it. Therefore, I bear witness, and all Thy creation beareth witness, that in every Revelation the Seat of the Manifestation of Thy Self is the most exalted of all the seats of Thy good-pleasure and the most resplendent of all the degrees of Thy Paradise.

Glory be unto Thee! How little do Thy creatures know Thee when they desire to circumambulate around the Sacred House instead of the Manifestation of Thy Self. For if the Sacred House were to circumambulate around the Manifestation of Thy Self—since their standing was solely through Thy word of old—then wherever the Manifestation of Thy Self is, that is the seat of Thy glory, not what was the seat of Thy glory aforetime which was the repository of Thy mystery; nay, wherever the Manifestation of Thy Self is, the seat of Thy glory revolveth around Him.

Glory be unto Thee, glory be unto Thee! And this pertaineth to the realm of limitations, else Thy Cause was not in the first nor in the last, but rather by that which establisheth the Prophethood of Thy Prophet is established the Manifestation of Thy Self. When He doeth what He willeth from His presence, that which was revealed aforetime returneth not unto Him, for what was revealed before was revealed according to what was ordained. This is a new creation and a fresh world, built from the beginning with a subtle structure and raised up from the moment of its mention with a lofty elevation.

And thus hast Thou mentioned that in every land wherein the Manifestation of Thy Self appeareth on the Day of Resurrection, this is the seat of Thy glory and the exaltation of Thy house, even though before the appearance of the Manifestation of Thy Self, that which was raised up from the lands was truth wherein there is no doubt. But when Thou dost manifest the Dawning-Place of Thy mystery, then that which Thou didst choose is what Thou choosest, and that which Thou didst select is what Thou selectest.

Glorified art Thou! There is no God but Thee; all stand by Thy command, and there is nothing from anything except through Thee. When His proof is established, that utterance is not that by which one would seek to prove, and after its verification, whosoever presenteth it to the Book shall find it in conformity therewith. And after the establishment of both, then it behooveth one to ask, but before that it is an utterance without proof and a mention without a way.

It beseemeth not the wise knower and the steadfast scholar to ask about something whose religion is not established with him. In every Manifestation, all that was ordained before is raised up anew. How then can particular affairs flow in particular instances? Verily, what Thou didst answer concerning that tradition was naught but a grace from Thy presence and a bounty from Thy side. And verily, like unto that which was mentioned after that word concerning creation, I found not the spirit of animality for one who seeketh the mystery of reality.

Hath man been lost that he should present animals before God? Glory be unto Thee, glory be unto Thee, above the imaginings of those who imagine concerning Thy proof! Nay, on the day when Thy proof spoke, He desired to manifest the mention of the animality of primary spirits, and He desired naught else, for the appearance of Thy proof was naught but the manifestation of Thy Self. Therefore, the essences of Thy creation must appear before Thee, and Thou hast not created anything closer than the praiseworthy manifestations of His self to appear before Thee. And he was not inexperienced in the affairs of sovereignty nor of knowledge, except when compared with knowledge of Thee and obedience unto Thee, for knowledge of Thee sufficeth the servant above all else, while all else sufficeth not in place of knowledge of Thee.

And as for what has been mentioned concerning the white bird, according to their various accounts, the first to pledge allegiance must be either Muhammad or Gabriel, and both of these are one and the same in the eyes of the people of truth. Therefore, O God, inspire him to consider the numerical value of the bird, for the number of the first to pledge allegiance is from Thy pure Name, reduced by one, so that those who seek proof may deduce that after the letter Ba stands in the station of Sin, and that it descends from Lordship through the oneness of the Primal Point. And the mention of whiteness is because it is mentioned in the pillar of glorification, and all this is mention of grace and exposition of bounty.

Otherwise, the essences of truth return to the essences of the gates of guidance, and the essences of the gates of guidance return to the Most Exalted Gates, and the essences of the Most Exalted Gates return to the essences of the Most Glorious Gates, and these return to the Manifestation of the Point, beyond which contingent beings cannot reach, where return unto Him is return unto God and beginning from Him is beginning from God. And when the decree of the essences of names is thus with thee, how then would their sayings be mentioned?

Therefore, O God, inspire all Thy creation with that to which all their evidence returns, for otherwise none shall be saved on the Day of Resurrection before Thee. And verily, I shall cite the example of creation as mirrors facing the sun: he who sees the sun in the fifth mirror deduces the sun’s radiance according to its appearance therein, but when he looks at the sun itself, he finds that its existence depends on it, let alone its radiance.

Glory be unto Thee! Glory be unto Thee! He who recognizes Thee on the day of Thy manifestation needs no such proof, much less what was created by Thy word in Thy manifestation, for what was created before in that manifestation by the word of Thy Beloved shall not be accepted with Thee except through what is created in the manifestation of Thy innovation. Glory be unto Thee! There is no God but Thee! From the first manifestation of the Manifestation of Thy Self until the Day of Resurrection, Thou callest out in all regions of the heavens and earth and what lies between them that truth is with Thy proof.

And I do not wish to mention the call of those beneath its shade, for the heaven with Thee is naught but the heaven of knowledge in the hearts of Thy loved ones, and the caller is none other than the temples of those who seek proof, and less than that which hath no reality with Thee and to which the possessors of knowledge among Thy creation pay no heed, for Thou shalt manifest all things that God hath ordained from Thy presence. And on the day when Thou didst manifest Thy Beloved aforetime, the first to proclaim His truth was Ali, Thy friend, until his call was raised up and every matter was exalted, such that it became hidden beyond the mention of truth where no mention remained with him. Thus is Thy command in every manifestation and Thy mystery in every concealment, for Thou hast not wished that any of Thy creation should believe in something imaginary, but rather see all things manifest and elevated.

And verily, concerning what hath been mentioned regarding the inheritance of the Prophets—if one were to reflect upon who the Prophet is, that His inheritance should be considered an inheritance—he would assuredly realize within himself that all the Messengers and their Books are, in their stations, prostrate before Thy face and worshipping Thy Being. And concerning all that Thou willest thereof, by Thy Word Thou createst what Thou desirest. Thy proof hath not been constrained by anything aforetime, for all that was created before was but evidence of His Manifestation, and likewise what they possessed was but evidence of what He possesseth. Beyond this there is no true meaning for what hath been revealed that could be verified by Thy witnesses of old. Otherwise, Thou createst what Thou pleasest by Thy Word and ordainest what Thou willest. Even as those who were mentioned in the Book before Muhammad were veiled by their belief in what their divines had ordained, so too are they who desire Thee not veiled now, though all that was revealed unto Thy spirit before Thy Beloved was, compared to what was revealed unto Thy Beloved, but as a drop compared to the ocean. Yet after this they became veiled by that drop from the vast, surging, and mighty sea.

Similarly, with respect to Thy Manifestation on the Day of Resurrection, that which was revealed over twenty-three years Thou canst, if Thou wishest, reveal in two days and nights if no interval separateth them. This is the revelation of Thy bounty, Thy bounty in the Hereafter, and the independence of Thy exaltation in the Manifestation of Truth.

Part 2: “There is no doubt that all…”

There is no doubt that all that is revealed from the Manifestation of Thy Self is that which is revealed from Thee. Thou didst reveal aforetime the word:

“Pilgrimage to the House is a duty unto God for all who are able to make the journey thereto.”

In every year, seventy thousand souls take shelter beneath the shade of that word, and this is due to the remoteness of creation; otherwise it behooveth all who are on earth to take shelter beneath its shade. Nay, this is but the limit of possibility; indeed, wert Thou to create the like of all that is on earth multiplied by all things, it would behoove them to take shelter beneath its shade, for Thy Cause deserveth this. But this hath not been made manifest due to the veils of Thy creation and the remoteness of Thy servants. Otherwise, verily Thy Cause hath dominion over all things, Thy knowledge encompasseth all things, and Thy power extendeth over all things.

The Messengers are but Thy servants, and the Books are but Thy signs.
