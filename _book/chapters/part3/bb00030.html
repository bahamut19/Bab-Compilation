BB00030: Answer to 24 questions of Mulla Ahmad

Part 1: “These are answers for the emigrant unto God…”

These are answers for the emigrant unto God, the One, the Single, the Self-Sufficient, the most noble and glorious Mulla Ahmad—upon him be the boundless and infinite splendor of his Lord. I beseech God to preserve him in His glory until He causes him to ascend to the eternal realm and raises him above that world and admits him into the everlasting paradise. Verily He has power over all things and knowledge of all things.

In the Name of God, the Most Unapproachable, the Most Holy

Glorified art Thou, O my God! I and all things bear witness that Thou art God, there is no God but Thee, alone with no partner. Thine is the kingdom and the dominion, Thine the might and the omnipotence, Thine the power and divinity, Thine the strength and majesty, Thine the sovereignty and the temporal realm. Thou givest life and causeth death, then causeth death and giveth life. Verily Thou art the Ever-Living Who dieth not, the King Who passeth not away, the Just Who oppresseth not, the Sovereign Who changeth not, the Single One from Whose grasp nothing escapeth, whether in the heavens or on the earth or between them. Thou createst what Thou willest by Thy command. Verily Thou hast power over all things.

I and all things bear witness that Thou canst not be praised by Thine Own Self, nor glorified by Thine Own Being, nor unified by Thine Own Nature, nor magnified by Thine Own Signs. I glorify Thee above all that hath or will prostrate before Thee, I sanctify Thee beyond all that hath or will sanctify Thee, I exalt Thee above all that hath or will unify Thee. I declare my separation from all whereupon the name of a thing can fall, for all besides Thee are Thy creation and within Thy grasp. Thou hast created all things by Thy Will and made them the sun of Thy Oneness. Thou hast ordained for them neither beginning save their own beginning nor end save their own ending. Through them Thou hast sent down the Books and raised up the Messengers, that none should be worshipped except Thee and none should be seen as an object of worship besides Thee.

I bear witness, O my God, that Thy proof hath been perfected unto all contingent beings through all things, and Thy Word hath been exalted in every time above all created things. I know no beginning whereby I might mention Thee, nor do I know an end whereby I might describe Thee as two. Rather, from the wondrous beginning of Thy creation until now, nothing hath been manifest from Thee except the sign of Thy Will, which Thou hast manifested in every Dispensation through the name of a Prophet, until the worlds attained unto the Day of Muhammad, Thy Beloved. Thou hast made its likeness before Thee as the likeness of the sun—if Thou shouldst cause it to rise countless times, it is still one sun, and if Thou shouldst cause it to set countless times, it is still one sun. And Thou hast ordained from it in every Manifestation what Thou hast loved, whereby Thy unitarians worship Thee and they who are detached turn unto Thee.

I bear witness before Thee and before all things that, after what Thou didst manifest aforetime and the revelation of the Furqan upon him, Thou didst conceal him behind the veils of the Throne and store him within the tabernacle of glory for a Day Thou hadst ordained for the manifestation of Thy proof. And Thou didst make the manifestation of Thy proof none other than like unto the manifestation of Thy Beloved One before, and Thou didst manifest him even as Thou didst manifest Thy Beloved One through verses that leave the learned powerless. And even as Thou didst preserve these until that year, Thou didst ordain no manifestation save through Thy manifestation, and no concealment save through Thy concealment.

I bear witness before Thee at this moment that whosoever was guided by the Furqan before, should he return to what Thou didst reveal aforetime in the Furqan and what was veiled therein from every soul, he would be able to traverse the path more swiftly than the twinkling of an eye, for all that those who speak of Islam say harks back to the words of Thy Beloved One before, and verily he confirmeth what Thou didst ordain for him of truth through what Thou didst reveal unto him of clear verses that leave powerless all who dwell in the kingdoms of earth and heaven. And I bear witness before Thee, O my God, that the manifestation of Thy proof was none other than like unto the manifestation of Thy Beloved One, for all that Thou didst exalt as Thou desired in the Furqan through what Thou didst reveal unto Thy Messenger, that Thou mightest judge at that time from Thy presence, for creation and command are Thine.

And I bear witness before Thee concerning what people attribute to me, that I commanded naught in any book reaching unto all people save the purification of the air, and after making mention of the exaltation of Thy Cause and the Furqan, I joined therewith mention of the confirmation of Thy judgment from before upon all who believed in Thee until Thy judgment should reach them. And until that time I commanded no one with any new judgment from Thy presence, and in all wherein people differed they moved only in what pertained to the air. Yea, I guaranteed that people would find that which Thou wouldst bestow upon them of Thy grace, even as was Thy way in every manifestation.

And besides this ordinance, if a tradition is related from Me regarding a limit, I do not confirm it. Indeed, I have sent down nine units from the number of all things (361) in a Book, and I have not permitted this to anyone lest they be tried on that Day with what would grieve their souls. This is what I have made you and all your creation witness to. And after one thousand two hundred and seventy years have passed from the appearance of Muhammad, the slanderers will slander Him as these people have done, so how much more regarding the first manifestation of Your secret and Your mystery! Therefore, O God, protect Your God-fearing friends that they may not depart from an ordinance except to enter into what You have ordained for them from Your presence, and that they may not accept what is related from anyone unless they witness a Book whose truth is assured by Your Proof, or they hear from Him what they are able to act upon. For other than this is not the way of the God-fearing nor the path of the meticulous ones.

And I answer the letter Alif and Dal at the beginning of what he has intimately called upon You in his prayer, that You have ever been self-sufficient in Your Essence, and how much more is Your independence from Your creation. You created nothing except that it should know You and worship You, and nothing can know You except through what You have made known through Your Proof, and none can be assured of Your Proof except through what You have given through Your Prophet. Therefore, I bear witness, and all things bear witness, that on that Day there was no Proof from You except Him of the Seven Letters, Your servant whom You made the Proof of Your Prophet beforehand, and made Him the Last through the manifestation of Your Firstness, and the Hidden through the concealment of Your Outwardness, for You are the First and there is nothing before You, and You are the Last and there is nothing after You, and You are the Outward and nothing is Your peer, and You are the Hidden and nothing is Your equal.

You have brought all things into being through Your Will and established them by themselves, not from anything, through Your power, and You have made all else that faces them like mirrors wherein are seen the examples of their effulgence. And through this all Your creation speaks of Your unity through what has shone forth from its light, and the essence of all Your servants bears assured witness that there is no god but You, the Mighty, the Beloved.

Furthermore, I bear witness that in each of Your manifestations, all imagine they have attained Your good-pleasure and thereby are veiled from meeting You, for if the covering were removed from the eyes of those who believed in Your Book aforetime, they would surely be among the most prostrating before You and the most worshipful in Your presence. But since You have decreed for Yourself that none shall enter the paradise of Your knowledge except who is sincere to You, and You cause to enter the fire of lack of Your knowledge those who worship You night and day yet are not sincere to You after You have completed Your proof against every soul that is veiled from Your worship what has not purified its essence for You, and accepts You who has turned to You completely in all the worlds of Your Command and creation. Were it not for this, none would depart in any manifestation from acknowledging what You have ordained, for all love Your good-pleasure, but none attains this except who has recognized the Manifestation of Your Cause and Your station.

Therefore I bear witness to You that the beginning of all things is from You through what appears from the Tree of Your love, and the return of all things is to You through what returns to the station of Your Cause and Your Proof with You. And if I seek forgiveness by my deservingness for anyone, it has no beginning or end, and if I seek Your forgiveness by Your deservingness, I am ashamed to mention this before the court of Your holiness. Forgiveness appears only through what does not fail, and I seek refuge with You from what You do not love, and through such my relation should not be cut off from You when I relate myself to You.

Therefore I beseech You, O You Who existed before all things, by You upon You, that You bestow Your good-pleasure upon me, for this is the ultimate paradise of my desire, and that You protect me from other than Your love. By Thy glory, it grieveth me in Thy presence at Thy commencement.

Then I mention Thee secondly in that which I have witnessed concerning Thee, that Thou hast permitted all things to worship and supplicate Thee, and hast guaranteed response to whomsoever turneth to Thee with his face, seeking the ways of Thy good-pleasure. Thou hast made my independence to be from naught save Thee and from being veiled from the seats of Thy Cause and the wonders of Thy wisdom, for Thou didst create me and all things for everlasting life. The fire and the light are sustained—this through ascent and this through descent. I testify unto Thee that on that Day Thy summoner shall be none other than he who hath believed in Thee and Thy proof, who hath verified Thy words and Thy Prophet, for there is no soul that doth not call upon Thee. Yet should there remain on that Day anyone from Thy wondrous creation who calleth upon Thee, how can his supplication profit him? For Thou hast loved to be worshipped and called upon in the way Thou lovest and art pleased with, not in the way Thy creation loveth and Thy servants are pleased with.

And when this one called upon Thee through the ways of Thy knowledge, Thou didst therefore answer him from Thy presence through Thy decree, that he might give thanks to his Lord on the day of His manifestation and not wish for his death through that which his knowledge encompasseth not. For were he to look upon the sweetness of Thy knowledge and be assured of the manifestation of Thy Self through Thy signs, he would see no station for himself above that in the highest paradise, nor would he have any excellence with Thee and with them who know Thee, save that he turneth through Thee as do Thy chosen ones. For that which Thou hast promised in the next world and that which Thou hast created in Paradise is for him upon whom Thou hast bestowed the favor of Thy knowledge in his life—else how could he taste after his death that which is beloved in Thy gardens?

By Thy might! Thou art the ultimate goal of my Paradise and my desire, Thou art the end of my delight and my hope. I find no sweetness save in Thee, and if I do find sweetness, then the very essence of my deed is my fire, requiring no other fire. For that which Thou hast promised in the Hereafter is the fruit of that deed. By Thy might! I seek Thy protection from that and take refuge in Thee from this. In Thee do I seek shelter and protection, in Thee do I take refuge and find strength, upon Thee do I rely and through Thee am I strengthened. There is no power nor strength save in Thee.

And that which hath been mentioned, O my God, concerning […] in this, that from the day Thou didst send down the Furqan until Thou didst raise it to the summit of the Bayan, all who have conformed to Thy good-pleasure have attained unto Thy presence—a number beyond counting from the first and the last. And all this returneth to the manifestation of Thy power in Thy Book, whereat all Thy creation is powerless. And on that Day Thou didst not permit that I should expound such words, for such words are attributed to the Manifestations of Thy Cause, and they and their words are attributed to Thy Beloved and His words, and His prophethood is established with Thee and with Thy creation through what Thou didst send down of Thy verses.

There is no doubt that these are the attributes of one of Thy chosen ones—how then can the Manifestation of Thy Self be known through the attributes of Thy creation? Thou art far exalted above being known through any other than Thyself or being described by aught beside Thee. Nay, all are known through Thee and are guided to Thee through what Thou hast manifested unto them through themselves. Glory be unto Thee! There is no God but Thee. Glory be unto Thee! I am of those who know.

All that hath been mentioned in the fourth is quickened by a single water, if thou wilt but look to the beginning of thy Cause and trace back all contradictions from every soul to Thy testimony and Thy Book. Verily, none knoweth Thy purpose save those near ones upon whom Thou hast bestowed from Thy knowledge. Only that which is conjoined with a proof from Thee can be established with any soul.

I testify that at this moment there is no proof for anyone except through Thee and Thy signs, and no knowledge save through what pointeth to Thee and speaketh of what Thou hast revealed in Thy words. I seek Thy protection and commend my loved ones to Thy safekeeping from names which Thou hast not wished to be mentioned. For all goodly names are in the tree of Thy love, drawing nigh unto it and returning unto it, while others are in a tree devoid of Thy love, proceeding therefrom and returning thereto. Verily […] in this limit. This is Thy darkness for those who know Thee not, and this is Thy light for those who know Thee. By this is religion established with Thee, and what brancheth forth therefrom of goodly things none can reckon save Thee, and none can comprehend its knowledge except Thee.

I testify that the first to believe in Thee is the tree of Thy love, and the first to be veiled from Thee by his self is that which is other than the tree of Thy love. I seek Thy protection therefrom and draw not nigh through the guidance of Thy self unto Thee.
